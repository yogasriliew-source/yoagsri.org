## Editorial constraints

1. **Keep current Yoga Sri garden hero and portrait in place** until confirming which exact Drive photograph should replace each. The current Japanese temple images are potentially meaningful personal travel content, but do not imply Tamil/South Indian lineage; do not use them as primary brand cues.
2. Preserve ivory manuscript background `#FAF7EF`, dark forest `#2F5D46`, muted gold `#C8A45D`, generous whitespace, fine frames, Chinese serif headings and original Kolam SVG icons.
3. Do not synthetically restyle, replace, or extend photographic content. For the review pack, images were only EXIF-orientation-corrected, resized and compressed for web; no scene changes or art filters.
4. Match display by role: Home landscape `3:2` or `16:9`, course environment `4:3`, posture `4:5` with entire body visible, retreat `16:9`, atmosphere `3:2`.
5. Put readable body copy on the adjacent paper panel, not over busy photographs. No heavy vignette, fake sepia or aggressive filters.
6. Source photo use/rights and recognizable participant consent should be confirmed before final release.
7. **Ayurveda clinic photographs are a gap.** Until a physician/venue and actual care photographs are confirmed, use symbolic Kolam + text; never misrepresent a generic yoga or spa image as a treatment.
8. The older [Visual Map spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1HmSWY2BHu18UXXCtCEsaC3YAzEkTAAgzJMnksFzkAi4/edit) proposed the Japan temple images for the hero. This new shortlist treats them as personal-story imagery rather than South Indian brand assets.
